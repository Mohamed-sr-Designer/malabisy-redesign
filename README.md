# Malabisy redesign — prototype

A clickable prototype of the redesigned Malabisy store: the Malabisy Label and the Marketplace in one bag and one checkout.

**Live preview:** https://mohamed-sr-designer.github.io/malabisy-redesign/

## What's inside
- **Store:** home, Malabisy Label, Marketplace with filters, Brands A–Z, brand and product pages, bag, 3-step checkout with discount codes and an invoice, My account with order tracking.
- **AI Stylist:** outfit, colour and size help from in-stock pieces; understands English, Arabic, Franco, typos and brand names.
- **Dashboard** (`admin.html`): orders, products, brands, customers, discounts, content and visual editor, AI stylist settings, store settings, team roles and activity log. Sign-in required.

## Notes
- Static site (HTML, CSS, JavaScript). No build step.
- Prototype only: changes and orders are saved in the visitor's own browser, and no payment is taken. Sign-in runs in the browser; a live store needs a server.
- Product data and photos come from the public malabisy.com catalogue.
